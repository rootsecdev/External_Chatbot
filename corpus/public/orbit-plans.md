Orbit Starter is $49 per seat per month and includes 3 satellite feeds, 30-day
telemetry retention and email support. Orbit Pro is $129 per seat per month and
adds unlimited feeds, 1-year retention, the alerting API and an 8x5 support SLA.
Orbit Enterprise is custom-priced and adds SSO/SCIM, audit export, a dedicated
CSM and a 99.95% uptime SLA. All plans are billed monthly or annually; annual
billing is discounted. Contact sales for an Enterprise quote.
