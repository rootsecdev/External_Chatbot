---
title: Orbit quickstart
classification: public
source: https://docs.northwind-dynamics.example/orbit-quickstart
---
Create an API token under Settings > Tokens, then point your collector at
https://ingest.northwind-dynamics.example/v1/telemetry with the token in the
Authorization header. Feeds appear in the console within about 60 seconds. The
free trial runs 14 days with no card required and is limited to one feed.
