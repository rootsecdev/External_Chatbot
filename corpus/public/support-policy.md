---
title: Support and refund policy
classification: public
source: https://northwind-dynamics.example/legal/support-policy
---
Orbit subscriptions may be cancelled at any time from the billing settings page
and remain active until the end of the paid term. Refunds are issued at
Northwind Dynamics' discretion for billing errors and for annual plans within 14
days of purchase. Refund decisions are made by the billing team; support agents
and automated assistants cannot approve a refund. Response targets are 2
business days on Starter, 8x5 next-business-day on Pro, and per contract on
Enterprise.
